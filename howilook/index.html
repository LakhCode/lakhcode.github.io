Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the face & outfit scores calculated? expand_more

Our vision pipeline uses Google Cloud Vertex AI to assess facial harmony, bone proportions, symmetry ratios, skin grooming, and outfit color balance. The score is a multi-dimensional aesthetic rating on a calibrated 1–10 scale.

What is Brutal Mode™? expand_more

Brutal Mode changes the commentary tone of the feedback, not the underlying score. It strips away polite diplomatic phrasing and provides candid, direct, unfiltered critiques for looksmaxxing enthusiasts.

Are my photos safe and private? expand_more

Yes. Photos are securely uploaded to our encrypted Supabase infrastructure and transmitted over HTTPS for inference. You can request deletion of individual scans or your entire account anytime from inside the app or via our data deletion page.

Can I try the app for free? expand_more

Yes! Every new account gets 3 free starter scans with zero credit card required. Flexible Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly plans are available if you want to track your glow-up over the long term.

Is Howilook available on iOS? expand_more

Howilook is currently available on Android via Google Play Early Access. An iOS version is actively in development and coming soon to the App Store.
